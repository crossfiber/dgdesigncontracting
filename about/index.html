Eduardo Gomez and DG Design Contracting

A design and remodeling company that specializes in finishes, run by the person who shows up to do the work.

What the company is

DG Design Contracting LLC is a Florida company, registered in 2022 and based in Lake Mary. It works on homes and businesses: preconstruction planning, conceptual and 3D design, construction management, and above all finishes. Painting, limewash, stain, sealer, accent walls. The finish is the part everyone sees, so it is the part Eduardo cares most about.

How Eduardo works

Collaboration and no surprises. You are involved from the first idea to the final touch, you know what is happening each day, and you get a clear written estimate before anything starts. His reviewers put it their own way:

  • “Eduardo showed up when expected and prepared. I was informed every step of the way.” Geraldine D.
  • “Always kept area clean and did more than agreed upon originally.” Cynthia F.
  • “He was always responsive to texts and responded to our questions immediately.” Carrie P.

The four steps

  • On-site consultation. Free and no-obligation, at your place.
  • Design and estimate. Your space assessed and a clear, complete estimate, with a design or 3D drawing when the project calls for one.
  • Prep and protect. Floors, furniture, glass and landscaping covered before the work starts.
  • Finishing touches. Final details and a walkthrough with you.
