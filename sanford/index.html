Houses in Sanford

Sanford has some of the oldest housing in Seminole County, around the historic downtown and Lake Monroe: brick, wood siding, porches and original doors. Older surfaces need more prep and the right product, and brick in particular is where limewash and mineral paint do their best work.

Newer neighborhoods further out are mostly stucco, which comes down to washing, crack repair and a good exterior paint.
