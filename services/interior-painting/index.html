What we paint

  • Walls and ceilings, including two-story foyers and stairwells
  • Baseboards, crown molding, and window and door trim
  • Interior doors and closets
  • Bathrooms, bedrooms and condos
  • Accent walls, from one bold color to a built pattern (see accent walls)

How we prep a room

Drop cloths go down first. Furniture gets moved or wrapped in plastic. Holes and cracks get patched and the trim gets taped. Then the paint goes on.

The room is picked up at the end of each day. One customer had us paint her main living area while she worked from home with two dogs. She wrote that we “always kept area clean and did more than agreed upon originally.”

Help with colors

Stuck between three grays? Bring your samples to the estimate. We do design work too, so helping you choose is part of the job.
