What gets painted

Anything inside that takes paint. The rooms that come up most in reviews are living areas, bathrooms, closets and ceilings, and the tall ones: two-story foyers and stairwells that need ladders and patience.

  • Walls and ceilings, including vaulted and two-story spaces
  • Baseboards, crown, window and door trim
  • Interior doors and closets
  • Accent walls, from a single color to a built board pattern (see accent walls)
  • Condos, bathrooms and closets

How a room gets done

Floors get drop cloths and furniture gets wrapped or moved. Holes and cracks get patched before any color goes on, and trim and hardware are taped off. At the end of each day the room is picked up so you can use your house.

Cynthia had him paint her main living area while she worked from home with two dogs. Her words: “Always kept area clean and did more than agreed upon originally.”
