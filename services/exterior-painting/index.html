What a repaint covers

  • Stucco walls, with cracks repaired first
  • Painted brick, or limewash on brick (see limewash)
  • Soffits, fascia and gutters
  • Window frames and trim
  • Front doors, French doors and garage doors
  • Patios, sidewalks and pool decks

How we prep

Florida sun and rain are hard on paint. So we pressure wash the house, fill the stucco cracks, and mask the windows and doors. Then we paint.

One customer called about a stucco crack. “Edu and his team had it fixed within the week,” she wrote.

Picking colors

Body, trim and door colors have to work together, and with your roof. Bring photos of houses you like. If your HOA has an approved color list, bring that too and we will price the job around it.
