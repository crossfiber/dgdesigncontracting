Why it comes out even

A wand alone leaves stripes. A rotary surface cleaner spins over the concrete at one height and cleans it in a single pass. That is what gives you one even color from the garage to the street.

After the wash

Clean concrete is the right time to refresh it. Customers have had sidewalks and patios repainted after washing, and pavers and wood can be sealed. Ask about it at the estimate.
