The front door is the first thing people see

A refinished entry door changes a house more than its size suggests. The door is cleaned and prepped, the hardware and glass are masked, and then it is stained or painted.

Fences and outdoor wood

Florida sun grays wood fast. A fence gets washed, dried and then stained, and the sealer protects it from sun and water.
