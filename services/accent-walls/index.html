Accent walls, planned and built

Geometric board walls, line walls and stairwell features. We lay the pattern out on your wall first, then build it, caulk every joint and paint it as one surface.

What we build

  • Diagonal geometric board patterns
  • Vertical and stepped line walls
  • Stairwell walls and window surrounds
  • Board-and-batten and paneled walls
  • One bold color on the right wall, when that is all a room needs

Planned before we cut

We do 3D and conceptual design too. So the pattern is planned around your outlets, windows and doorways before a board is cut, and the lines land clean instead of running into a switch plate.

How it is finished

Boards go up, nail holes get filled, every joint gets caulked. Then the whole wall is painted at once, so it looks like part of the room.
