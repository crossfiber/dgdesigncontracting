What he builds

  • Geometric diagonal board patterns

  • Board-and-batten and paneled walls
  • A single bold color on the right wall, when that is all a room needs

Designed before it is built

DG Design Contracting does 3D design and conceptual design as well as the build. On an accent wall that means the pattern is laid out for your wall, with its outlets, windows and doorways, before a single board is cut. You see it first.

Finished like a wall, not a craft project

The boards are set, nail holes filled, every joint caulked, and then the whole wall is painted together so the pattern reads as part of the room.
