What limewash is

Limewash and mineral paint are made from lime and minerals rather than plastic resins. They bond with the masonry and let it breathe, which is why they suit brick in a humid climate. The finish is soft and matte, with the brick’s character showing through instead of being covered up.

Eduardo works with Romabio mineral products. Limewash can go on as a solid, even color, or be worked back while wet for a more weathered, old-world look.

What it goes on

  • Bare brick homes
  • Previously painted brick, when the existing paint allows it
  • Brick columns, planters and garden walls

Most limewash jobs also include the trim: Carrie had him limewash the brick and paint all the window frames and soffits in the same job.
